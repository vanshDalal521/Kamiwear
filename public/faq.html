01 / Orders

Orders & payment

Which payment methods do you accept?+
UPI and Cash on Delivery are available at checkout. Card payments appear only when the payment provider has enabled them for your region. We never store full card numbers on Kamiwear servers.
Can I change or cancel an order?+
Email support@kamiwear.com as soon as possible. We can usually change or cancel an order before it is dispatched, typically within 2 hours. Once the parcel is handed to the delivery partner, the exchange process applies.
How do I track my order?+
Sign in and open your Identity Terminal to see order history and tracking updates. If tracking has not moved for 48 hours, contact support with your order ID.
Do you offer gift cards or store credit?+
Store credit is issued for approved refunds when you choose the credit option. It never expires and can be applied at checkout. Physical gift cards are not currently available.
Can I use more than one promo code?+
One promo code can be applied per order. Codes cannot be combined with archive-sample pricing or other automatic drop discounts unless the promotion says otherwise.

02 / Shipping

Shipping & delivery

When will my order ship?+
Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Drops and personalised pieces can take an extra 1–2 days. You will receive a tracking link once the parcel leaves our studio.
How much does shipping cost?+
India: free over ₹2,999, otherwise ₹149. International: free over ₹12,000; below that, shipping is calculated at checkout. Customs duties and import taxes are the recipient's responsibility.
Do you ship internationally?+
Yes. International delivery usually takes 7–14 business days after dispatch. Some remote destinations can take longer. If your country is not available at checkout, email support and we will check a manual route.
My parcel says delivered but I cannot find it.+
Check with neighbours, building security and the delivery partner first. If the parcel is still missing after 24 hours, email us with the order ID and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

03 / Exchanges

Exchanges & returns

How long do I have to exchange an item?+
7 calendar days from delivery. The item must be unworn, unwashed and have its original tags attached. Start the request from your order history or email support@kamiwear.com.
Is the first size exchange free?+
Yes for apparel orders shipped within India. Subsequent size swaps or exchanges for a different product may carry a ₹149 handling fee. International return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item was damaged or incorrect.
Can I return a final-sale item?+
Final-sale, archive-sample, innerwear, earrings, masks and personalised pieces are not eligible unless they arrived damaged or incorrect. The full list is on the Exchanges page.
How long does a refund take?+
Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the quality check. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by UPI or bank transfer. Store credit is issued instantly once approved.

04 / Sizing

Sizing & fit

How do oversized pieces fit?+
Oversized hoodies and tees are cut with extra room through the chest and shoulders. If you want a cleaner fit, size down once. If you want the full streetwear drape, stay true to size.
How should I choose denim?+
Our wide-leg and baggy denim is designed to sit lower on the waist. Check the fit notes on each product page and compare your waist measurement to the size chart. When between sizes, choose the larger size for a relaxed silhouette.
What if the size I need is sold out?+
We can hold an exchange request for a restock, issue store credit, or refund the item. Contact support after raising the exchange and we will confirm the available option.

05 / Identity

Identity Terminal

What is the Identity Terminal?+
It is your Kamiwear account hub: sign in, view orders, manage your wishlist and track KamiKoins. The full guide is on the Identity Terminal page.
I logged out and cannot find the sign-in form.+
After logout, Kamiwear returns you to the page with a sign-in prompt and your email prefilled. If the prompt is closed, open the profile icon in the navigation or go to register.html and choose Sign In.
How long does a session last?+
A signed-in session lasts up to 7 days. The browser stores an httpOnly session cookie, which JavaScript cannot read. Closing the browser does not immediately sign you out; logging out does.
How do I reset my password?+
Self-serve password reset is not live yet. Email support@kamiwear.com from the address on your account and support will help you regain access. Never share your password in an email.
Why is the Google sign-in button missing?+
Google Sign-In appears only when the store owner has configured a Google client ID. When it is not configured, use the email and password form. Both routes create the same Kamiwear identity.

06 / Products

Products & care

How should I wash a graphic hoodie or tee?+
Turn the piece inside out, wash cold on a gentle cycle and hang dry in the shade. Do not iron directly over the print, and avoid bleach or fabric softener on heavyweight cotton.
Will the colours look exactly like the photos?+
We shoot in natural and studio light, but screens vary. Small differences in tone, wash and print placement are normal for garment-dyed and small-batch pieces.
Are drops restocked?+
Some core pieces return; limited capsule drops usually do not. Join the newsletter and follow the collection pages to hear about restocks and new chapters.

07 / Contact

Still need help?

Send your order ID and a short description to support@kamiwear.com. We reply within one business day, Monday to Saturday, 10:00–19:00 IST.
