05 / Shipping

Delivery

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. India delivery usually takes 3–7 business days; international delivery usually takes 7–14 business days. These windows are estimates, not guarantees.

Risk passes to you on delivery. If a parcel is lost or damaged in transit, contact us within 48 hours of the delivery scan so we can open a carrier investigation. Duties, taxes and customs fees on international orders are the recipient's responsibility unless stated otherwise.

Exchanges and returns

The exchange policy is set out on the Exchanges & Returns page and forms part of these terms. The standard window is 7 days from delivery, and items must be unworn, unwashed and have their original tags attached.
