01 / Collection

What we collect

Kamiwear collects the information needed to run the storefront and fulfil orders:

  • Identity data: name, email address, hashed password and optional Google account identifier.
  • Order data: items, sizes, quantities, prices, order status and shipping address.
  • Payment references: payment method, transaction ID and refund status. We do not store full card numbers or security codes.
  • Support data: messages, photos and order IDs you send to support.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, timestamps and security events used for rate limiting and troubleshooting.
  • Local preferences: cart, wishlist and remembered email stored in your browser, as described in the Cookie Policy.

02 / Use

How we use it

  • Create and secure your Identity Terminal account.
  • Process orders, payments, deliveries, exchanges and refunds.
  • Provide order history, wishlist and KamiKoin features.
  • Respond to support requests and resolve disputes.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, scraping and unauthorised access.
  • Improve storefront performance, product information and checkout reliability.
  • Send transactional messages about orders and, if you opt in, drop announcements.

03 / Sharing

Who we share with

We share only what is needed to run the service:

  • Payment and UPI providers to process and refund payments.
  • Delivery partners to ship orders and arrange reverse pickups.
  • Email and hosting providers that operate transactional mail and the storefront infrastructure.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where required by law or to protect Kamiwear's rights.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share data with advertising networks in the current build.

04 / Cookies

Cookies and local storage

The storefront uses an httpOnly kw_session cookie for authentication and browser local storage for the cart, wishlist and a small profile cache. The full list, purposes and lifetimes are in the Cookie Policy.

05 / Retention

How long we keep data

  • Account data: while your identity is active, then deleted or anonymised after a verified deletion request, subject to legal obligations.
  • Order and tax records: as long as required by applicable accounting and consumer law.
  • Support messages: up to 24 months after the request is closed.
  • Security logs: typically 30–90 days, unless needed for an investigation.
  • Browser storage: until you log out, clear site data or your browser removes it.

06 / Security

How we protect it

Kamiwear uses hashed passwords, an httpOnly SameSite session cookie, HTTPS in production, rate limiting, request sanitisation and CSRF protection. Access to production data is limited to people who need it to operate the store.

No system is perfect. If you suspect unauthorised access, email support@kamiwear.com immediately and change your password once support confirms the account is secure.

07 / Rights

Your controls

Subject to applicable law, you can ask to:

  • access the personal information linked to your account;
  • correct inaccurate profile or shipping details;
  • delete your account and associated personal data;
  • export a copy of your account and order history;
  • withdraw marketing consent without affecting transactional messages.

Email support@kamiwear.com with the subject Privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You can also clear browser storage yourself at any time.

08 / Children

Children's privacy

Kamiwear is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you are under 18, use the store only with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

09 / Transfers

International processing

Kamiwear is operated from India. Some service providers may process data in other countries. Where required, we use contractual safeguards and limit transfers to what is necessary to provide the storefront, payments, delivery and support.

10 / Changes

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as the storefront or the law changes. The effective date at the top of the page will change. Material updates will be announced on the site or by email where appropriate.

11 / Contact

Privacy contact

Email support@kamiwear.com with the subject Privacy request. Support replies within one business day, Monday to Saturday.
