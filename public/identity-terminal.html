01 / Overview

What the Identity Terminal is

The Identity Terminal is Kamiwear's account system. It links your orders, wishlist, KamiKoins and profile to one email address, without turning your session into a localStorage free-for-all.

You can browse, add to cart and build a wishlist as a guest. When you are ready, sign in or create an identity to keep the archive connected across visits.

03 / Capabilities

What you can do

  • Open your dashboard. See your profile, tier and KamiKoin balance at profile.html.
  • Track orders. Order history and status are tied to your identity, not a browser cache.
  • Keep a wishlist. Save pieces from any product card and return to them from the wishlist page.
  • Earn KamiKoins. Your XP balance appears in the terminal and grows with activity.
  • Move between devices. Sign in with the same email to recover your profile and order history. Guest cart and wishlist items live in the browser, so they do not travel automatically.

04 / Security

How the session works

Kamiwear keeps authentication deliberately boring:

  • The server issues an httpOnly session cookie named kw_session. It cannot be read by JavaScript.
  • The cookie is SameSite=Lax, scoped to the site path, and marked Secure in production.
  • Sessions last up to 7 days. Logging out clears the cookie and the local profile cache.
  • Only the public profile is cached in localStorage for fast UI. The JWT is never stored there.
  • The server applies rate limiting, request sanitisation and CSRF protection to state-changing requests.
  • Passwords are hashed; Kamiwear never stores plaintext passwords.

05 / Troubleshooting

Common fixes

I logged out and the sign-in form disappeared.+
Logout returns you to the same page with ?auth=true and opens the sign-in view. If you closed it, click the profile icon in the navigation or visit register.html and choose Sign In. Your last email is prefilled.
My session ended unexpectedly.+
Sessions expire after 7 days, after a server-side session revocation, or when the cookie is cleared. Sign in again with your email and password. If it keeps happening, check that your browser is not blocking cookies for this site.
I forgot my password.+
Self-serve reset is not live yet. Email support@kamiwear.com from the address on the account and support will help. Never send your password in an email.
My cart or wishlist vanished.+
Guest cart and wishlist data are stored in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data, using private mode, or switching devices will make them appear empty. Signed-in order history is stored server-side and is not affected.
Why is Google Sign-In not visible?+
It appears only when a Google client ID is configured for the deployment. When it is absent, use email and password. Both routes resolve to the same Kamiwear identity.
