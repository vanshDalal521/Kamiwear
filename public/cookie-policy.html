01 / Scope

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Kamiwear uses cookies, localStorage and sessionStorage on the storefront. It should be read with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

02 / Basics

Cookies and browser storage

A cookie is a small text file a site asks your browser to store. Local storage and session storage are browser databases that hold larger values, such as your cart, wishlist and cached profile. None of these are programs and none can read other files on your device.

03 / Essential

Essential storage

These are required for sign-in, checkout and the basic storefront to work:

Name Type Purpose Lifetime
kw_session Cookie Signed-in session. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure in production. Up to 7 days
kw_user localStorage Public profile cache for instant UI. Contains no JWT. Until logout or cleared
kw_cart localStorage Your cart, including guest carts. Until cleared
kw_wishlist localStorage Saved products. Until cleared
kw_session_hint localStorage Signals that a server session may exist, so the UI can verify it. Until logout or cleared
kw_session_verified_at localStorage Timestamp used to avoid repeated session checks. Until logout or cleared

04 / Preferences

Convenience keys

Name Type Purpose Lifetime
kw_last_email localStorage Prefills your email after logout or on the sign-in form. Until cleared
kw_signed_out sessionStorage Shows the "session ended" notice once after logout. Current tab session
kw_redirect_after_login sessionStorage Returns you to the page you were on after signing in. Current tab session
kw_token localStorage Legacy JWT cleanup only. New sessions never write a token here; the key is removed when a session is stored or cleared. Removed on session change

05 / Analytics

Analytics and advertising

The current Kamiwear build does not load advertising pixels or a third-party analytics script. If analytics is added later, this policy will be updated and, where required, consent will be requested before non-essential storage is set.

Server logs may record request metadata such as IP address, browser type and timestamps for security and troubleshooting. That data is described in the Privacy Policy.

06 / Third parties

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts and Material Symbols load fonts and icons from Google's CDN. Google may receive your IP address and request metadata.
  • Tailwind CDN serves the CSS runtime used by the storefront.
  • Google Identity Services loads only when a Google client ID is configured and you choose to sign in with Google. Google may set its own cookies during that flow.
  • Payment and delivery partners may set cookies or collect data on their own domains during checkout or tracking.
  • Social links to Discord, Instagram, TikTok and Twitter are ordinary links. They do not load embedded trackers unless you click through.

07 / Managing

How to control storage

You can clear or block cookies and browser storage in your browser settings. Clearing Kamiwear site data will:

  • sign you out of the Identity Terminal;
  • empty a guest cart and wishlist stored in this browser;
  • remove the remembered email used to prefill sign-in;
  • not delete server-side order history tied to your account.

Blocking the kw_session cookie prevents sign-in and checkout. Because Kamiwear does not load non-essential analytics or ad cookies, there is currently no consent banner to manage.

08 / Updates

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the storefront adds new storage or third-party services. The effective date at the top of the page will change. Material changes will be announced on the site or by email where appropriate.

09 / Contact

Questions about cookies

Email support@kamiwear.com with the subject Cookie question. Support replies within one business day, Monday to Saturday.
