01 / Window

The 7-day window

Your exchange window opens when the delivery partner marks your order as delivered. You have 7 calendar days to request an exchange from the Identity Terminal order history or by emailing support@kamiwear.com.

Requests raised after the window are reviewed case by case. We cannot guarantee approval once the window has closed, so start the request as soon as you know the fit is not right.

02 / Eligibility

What qualifies

To keep the archive clean and fair, exchanged pieces must come back in resale-ready condition:

  • The item is unworn, unwashed and unused.
  • All original tags, labels and hygiene seals are attached.
  • The item is returned in its original packaging or equivalent protective packaging.
  • No perfume, deodorant, makeup, smoke, pet hair or washing detergent is present.
  • Footwear is tried on indoors only and shows no creasing, scuffs or outdoor wear.
  • The request is raised within 7 days of delivery.

Pieces that fail inspection are returned to you. If you want them shipped back, a delivery fee may apply.

03 / Exclusions

What we cannot exchange

For hygiene, safety and inventory reasons, the following are final sale unless they arrived damaged or incorrect:

  • Innerwear, socks, face masks and earrings.
  • Personalised or made-to-order pieces.
  • Items marked Final Sale or Archive Sample at checkout.
  • Mystery bundles and opened blind-box products.
  • Gift cards and digital products.

04 / Process

Start an exchange

  1. Open your terminal. Sign in at the Identity Terminal and open Order History, or email us with your order ID.
  2. Tell us what changed. Share the item name, the size you need and a quick photo if the piece is damaged or incorrect.
  3. Approve the pickup. We arrange a reverse pickup with our delivery partner. Keep the item packed and the pickup phone number reachable.
  4. Quality check. Once the piece reaches our studio, we inspect it within 48 hours.
  5. Replacement dispatched. Approved exchanges ship within 3–5 business days. If your size is unavailable, we offer store credit or a refund.

You can also start by emailing support@kamiwear.com with the subject line Exchange request.

05 / Size swaps

Size swaps, without the drama

The first size swap on apparel orders shipped within India is free. Subsequent size swaps, or exchanges for a different product, may carry a ₹149 handling and reverse-pickup fee.

If the size you need is out of stock, we can hold the request for a restock, issue store credit, or process a refund. We will confirm the available option before closing the request.

Need help choosing? The sizing FAQ includes fit notes for denim, hoodies and oversized tees.

06 / Damaged

Damaged or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, report it within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos of the item, packaging and shipping label. We cover return shipping and send a replacement or refund, including any delivery fee.

Do not wash or alter a damaged piece before contacting us. Alterations can make a claim difficult to verify.

07 / Shipping

Shipping costs and timelines

Domestic orders over ₹2,999 ship free. Below that threshold, standard shipping is ₹149. International orders over ₹12,000 ship free; below that, shipping is calculated at checkout.

  • Dispatch: 1–2 business days after payment confirmation.
  • India delivery: 3–7 business days after dispatch.
  • International delivery: 7–14 business days after dispatch.
  • Duties, taxes and customs fees are the recipient's responsibility unless stated otherwise.

For damaged or incorrect items, reverse pickup is free. For change-of-mind exchanges, the fee depends on your location and is confirmed before pickup.

08 / Refunds

Refund timelines

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the quality check. Refunds go back to the original payment method where possible. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by UPI or bank transfer using the details you provide.

Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect. Store credit is issued instantly once approved and never expires.

09 / Contact

Still stuck?

Email support@kamiwear.com with your order ID and a short description of the issue. Support replies within one business day, Monday to Saturday. For account questions, open the Identity Terminal guide.
